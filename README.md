# HOLLOW WARD – Escape the Matron

A free horror escape game for phone browsers (Android-first, works on desktop too). Part of LeonLink Arcadeline.

## How to play
- Escape 3 floors: B1 Intake, F2 The Loud Ward, F3 The Matron's Roof.
- Find the red, blue and gold keys to open the locked doors, then reach the exit.
- Hide in lockers when you hear the Matron humming. Your flashlight helps you see but lets her spot you from farther away. Running is loud.

## Controls
- Phone: left side of the screen = move joystick, drag on the right = look, on-screen buttons for Use, Light, Hint and Pause.
- Keyboard: WASD/arrows to move, mouse drag to look, E/Space use, F light, H hint, Esc pause.

## Tech
Single `index.html`, no build step, no dependencies, synthesized audio. Deploys as a static site on Vercel.
